Lift = shot rate when a flag is True minus shot rate when it is False, in percentage points, against the dataset base rate of 5.97%. Skew is the sample skewness (scipy.stats.skew) of the full (unclipped) column. Continuous histograms use 24 equal-width bins from the column minimum to its 99th percentile; rows above p99 are counted as "extreme" but never dropped from the reported mean/median/skew. Boolean groups (True or False) with n < 500 are flagged as small-n rather than excluded. All findings describe correlation/association, never causation.
