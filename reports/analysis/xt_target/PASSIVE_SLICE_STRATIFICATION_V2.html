Reading the curves. ADAPTATION 3, unchanged in method and re-measured on the passive leg. Sign carries the football meaning: POSITIVE target_xt_delta_passive = xT fell across the event (threat reduced, good for the defence), NEGATIVE = xT rose. On this leg the two directions are almost exactly balanced -- 36.4% positive vs 37.2% negative -- but the target is strongly LEFT-skewed (skew -2.6036), far more so than active v2's -0.306, so the negative tail is much longer than the positive one even though the counts match. Two-directional framing therefore matters more here than anywhere else in this portal. Rankings are by |magnitude| across BOTH directions, and every table also reports the negative/positive/zero share behind its mean. Every sparkline carries a dashed zero baseline, which the xg report has no need for -- its values are non-negative, so min-max scaling alone conveys the shape; here a curve entirely below zero and one entirely above it would otherwise look identical. flat_margin on this run: 0.002736 unconditional, 0.003187 on the non-zero-delta subset.
