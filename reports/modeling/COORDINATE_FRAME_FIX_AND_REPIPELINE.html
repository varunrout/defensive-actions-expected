Background

COORDINATE_FRAME_IMPACT_AUDIT.html (prompt 84) is the full diagnosis this prompt executes against: 4 of 6 legs have frame-invariant targets (feature-only exposure, 36.6-84.3% of importance/weight on frame-dependent features per leg); the 2 xT legs (x1c_random_forest, y1c_random_forest) have directly frame-dependent targets, exposed for 69.13% / 67.19% of their own training rows. Phase 1 fixed _infer_attack_sign_by_period_team (noisy per-(period, possession_team) inference) with a row-local rule, _attack_sign_for_row(team, possession_team): sign=+1 if team == possession_team (already the acting team's own frame), sign=-1 otherwise -- confirmed by a goalkeeper sanity oracle (92.8% of GK defensive actions sit at raw x<20 in their own frame, exactly where a keeper's own action should be).

Phase 2 -- Re-run feature engineering

Full pipeline re-run once for all 6 legs: scripts/run_pipeline.py --stage all (raw-data refresh, enrichment through the fixed loader, event-context, phase labelling, the two frame-invariant short-horizon targets), then scripts/features/build_passive_defense_dataset.py and scripts/features/add_missingness_flags.py (a second pass was required after the first rebuild silently dropped the missingness-flag columns on both feature tables -- restored and re-validated against that script's own hardcoded expected-false-count sanity gate, which passed unchanged).

Phase 4 -- Re-train and re-evaluate the 4 non-xT legs

Scope decision. Each promoted model is refit at its already-locked hyperparameters (no re-tuning), on the identical 5-fold canonical CV, compared via the same paired test (ttest_rel + Wilcoxon) each leg's own promotion already used. Re-running the full historical grid-search ladder was rejected: it would conflate "did the fix change what's optimal" with "did the fix change how good the promoted model is", and the prompt's own scope excludes the full ladder.

Phase 5 -- Fresh promotion audit, the 2 xT legs

Both xT targets are directly recomputed from corrected coordinates, so this is not a before/after diff of the same ground truth. The old comparator rungs' (x1_two_stage_huber / y1_two_stage_huber) held-out numbers were scored against the now-provably-wrong target and are not reused for the decision -- instead both candidate and comparator are refit from scratch on the corrected target, at their own already-locked configurations, on identical 5-fold canonical CV.

Promotion verdicts

Active-xT: PROMOTE x1c_random_forest. Fresh promotion decision on genuinely new target values, run as a full audit: x1c beats x1_two_stage_huber by a wide, highly significant margin when both are refit on the corrected target. Headline numbers update to RMSE=0.04630/R²=0.37407.

Passive-xT: PROMOTE y1c_random_forest. Same pattern: y1c beats y1_two_stage_huber decisively on the corrected target. Headline numbers update to RMSE=0.02418/R²=0.33130.

Neither leg promotes a different rung than before. The fix changes the number materially (both R² drop by roughly a fifth to a third of their old value), but not the choice of which architecture to promote.
