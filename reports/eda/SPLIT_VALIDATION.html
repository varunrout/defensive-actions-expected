method

Per match, per leg, target_future_shot_10s rate; combined as the average of each leg's own percentile rank (not either leg's raw rate alone) into one combined_rank per match. train_test_split(matches, test_size=0.20, stratify=qcut(combined_rank, 4), random_state=42) -> TEST/TRAIN+VAL. StratifiedGroupKFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=42) on the TRAIN+VAL pool, grouped by match_id, stratified on the row-level target (active leg).
