Cramer's V (bias-corrected, src.eda.correlation.cramers_v) for the 6 genuinely categorical features, since variance-ratio has no natural definition for a non-numeric feature -- same 0-1 scale and the same 0.3 threshold, but a different statistic (chi-square association, not a variance decomposition); each row states which method produced its value. Threshold: 0.3 (same convention as prompt 27's Cramer's V thresholds).

Step 3 -- train/test player overlap

23 test matches (479 distinct players) vs 92 train+val matches (964 distinct players). 89.98% of test-set players (431 of 479) also appear in train+val. Some overlap is expected and not inherently a problem -- players play in multiple matches across this 115-match, 2-tournament dataset. The percentage itself, not an assumption about it, is what matters for Step 3's risk-pair cross-reference.
